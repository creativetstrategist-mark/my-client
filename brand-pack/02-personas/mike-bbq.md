# MIKE | The BBQ Guy / Grill Master

**Notion `Avatar` value:** `MIKE- BBQ KING`
**Focus products:** LOKI Blackout (primary), Feather
**YTD:** $67,638 spend · 1.50 ROAS

Sarah's husband. The man who cooks and grills — big beard, big build, looks like
a Viking. For him the marketing can go harder: a deeper, darker ElevenLabs
voice, rock music, even hard rock.

## Core trigger

Mike doesn't need convincing that he deserves a good knife. He needs convincing
that **this** knife is worth it.

In evergreen periods there is no seasonal event to give him permission. The
creative must give him **identity permission** instead: *this is the kind of man
who owns this knife. Is he that man?*

## Profile

- **Age** 30–55 · middle to upper-middle · **self-buyer, always** — he is buyer
  and user simultaneously.
- **Hormone:** Testosterone primary (performance, craft mastery, status among
  peers), Dopamine secondary (he already searched "best BBQ knife" before the ad
  found him; real product information is the reward).
- **NOT oxytocin or cortisol dominant.** "He'll love it" lands in the wrong
  universe — *he* is the he. Manufactured urgency destroys trust immediately. He
  is sceptical of marketing by default; only evidence overcomes it.
- **Colour:** Red primary (acts once decided, needs one great piece of evidence,
  not seven touchpoints), Blue secondary (quality-obsessive, notices when a
  brand knows what it's talking about).

He needs both the feeling *and* the facts. Show him — don't tell him.

## Responds to / recoils from

| Responds TO | Recoils FROM |
|---|---|
| Men who look like him using it | Soft, gifting-focused language |
| Product truth — real material, real craft | Vague superlatives |
| Viking identity and heritage language | Overly polished, influencer-coded |
| Extreme close-ups of blade texture | Countdown timers, fake urgency |
| Performance evidence — cutting real things | "He'll love it" / wife-buyer framing |
| First-person owner voice ("my go-to blade") | Heavy brand voice with no authenticity |
| Outdoor, BBQ, grill settings — his world | Domestic-kitchen-only content |
| The dramatic Viking identity pose | Generic "perfect gift" language |
| Dismissal of inferior knives | Aggressive discount-first hooks |
| Shame hook naming his situation directly | Flash-sale energy — cheapens it |
| Real scarcity on limited editions | Manufactured pressure tactics |
| VO / AI-VO over cinematic outdoor B-roll | Female talking-head as primary narrator |

## Language

**Words that convert:** go-to blade · handcrafted · Viking · brutal durability ·
hammer finish · finger loop · cleaves · backyard legend · feel like a savage ·
second-rate blades · embarrassment · pitmaster · built for this · worthy ·
extension of your hand · outlast your grill · nothing stops it · upgrade

**Sentence patterns:**
- "Swinging a dull kitchen knife at the grill is a recipe for embarrassment"
- "This blade doesn't cut it. It cleaves."
- "Grip it like a pro. Feel like a savage."
- "Slice your meat, pop your beer, never miss a beat."
- "One tool to rule the backyard."
- "Stop butchering your BBQ with second-rate blades."
- "It's rugged, slick, and built to outlast your grill."
- "I've used a lot of knives. This one is different."

**Towards-bridges:**
- "That gives you the control to make every cut count"
- "That enables you to handle anything — prep, carve, outdoors"
- "That makes you the guy with the knife everyone asks about"

**Highest-trust word:** **brutal** — it signals honesty about the product rather
than marketing polish. Mild profanity works for the same reason: it names his
frustration in his own language and signals the content is talking *to* him, not
*at* him.

**Tone:** confident, direct, slightly laconic. Doesn't oversell. Sounds like a
man recommending something to a friend.
*"I own this. It's the real thing. Draw your own conclusions."*

## Avoid

Gift-framing ("perfect for him", "surprise him") · soft seasonal urgency ·
over-explaining the offer (he is not primarily deal-driven) · female talking-head
as primary narrator · domestic-only settings · specs without demonstration.
