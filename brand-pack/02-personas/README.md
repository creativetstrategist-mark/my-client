**Copy the Avatar strings literally.** The spacing is irregular
(`MIKE- BBQ KING`, `VANCE- The Opportunist`) and these are the actual select
option names in the database.

Out of scope: STEVE (Homecook), DARIEN (Proud Parents), DANA (Knife Sharpener),
DON (Hunter), COLE (Camper/Bushcrafter). DON and COLE are newer and still marked
"to be validated" in Notion.

## Read these as register guides, not as evidence

Every page here carries a ⚠️ block listing where it contradicts the shipped ad
corpus. The standing rule from the brand's own performance review: **where a
persona doc conflicts with the real ad corpus, the corpus wins.** Full conflict
list in `05-process/creative-brief-process.md` §C.6.
