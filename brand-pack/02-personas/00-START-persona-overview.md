## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Hand-forged, Viking-inspired knives — for the kitchen, the grill, and the collection.
**What it does:** NorthernKnife sells hand-forged carbon-steel knives across five lines — All-in-One Knives, Bushcraft & Camping, Cooking Knives, BBQ Knives, and Collectible Knives — plus sets, sheaths, cutting boards, and sharpeners. Product naming and world-building is built entirely on Norse/Viking mythology (Loki, Odin, Ragnar, Ragnarok, Bjorn, Valhalla).
**Product category:** Direct-to-consumer premium/heritage knives (kitchen + outdoor + collector crossover)
**Product type:** E-commerce / DTC

## Target Audience
Five buyer personas, each with a distinct trigger. Full detail in `personas-in-full.md`.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Buy a gift for a man that will actually get used and admired (Sarah)
- Get an exceptional deal / stack value across multiple recipients (Vance)
- Own a knife that signals identity and mastery at the grill (Mike)
- Add a rare, artfully-made piece to a collection (John)
- Replace starter kitchen knives with a "researched and proven" upgrade (Steve)

## Problems & Pain Points
- **Sarah:** buying gifts for men is genuinely hard; fear of another gift that sits unused in a drawer.
- **Vance:** wants to feel smart about a purchase, not "cheap" — needs the math to justify spending more than planned.
- **Mike:** tired of his knife embarrassing him at his own cookout; needs identity permission, not convincing that knives matter.
- **John:** has seen it all; needs something genuinely rare he hasn't already got, or he won't bite.
- **Steve:** has done the research and is choosing between the last few contenders; needs the tie-breaking proof point.

## Objections & Anti-Personas
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Is this actually made in China / mass-produced?" | Lead with the real forging-process story (carbon-block stretching, hand assembly) — a legitimate process claim, not flavor text. |
| "Will it actually stay sharp?" | Long-term user proof points ("held an edge for 7 years, never sharpened") + full-tang construction. |

**Anti-persona:** bargain shoppers who want the cheapest possible blade with no story attached.

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** two registers, blended — (1) mythic/heroic for product names and hooks ("Command every cut. Conquer every challenge."), (2) warm, reassuring customer-service voice for guarantees/FAQ ("0 Questions Asked," "our lovely team"). Ad copy sits in register 1; trust/offer/FAQ copy sits in register 2 regardless of persona.

## Proof Points
- 6,644 published customer reviews (US + UK combined), average rating **4.93 / 5** — 94% are 5-star.
- **Confirmed ad-safe claims: "5,000+ reviews" and "100,000+ happy customers."**
- Free shipping threshold is market-dependent: **US $100+**, **UK £40+** (cross-check against brand-bible.md's $30+/$75+ note — confirm current number before using in copy).
- Documented hand-forging process (carbon-balancing, hand assembly) is a real production-method proof point, not just marketing flavor.
- 30-Day Risk-Free "0 Questions Asked" guarantee.

## Goals
**Key conversion action:** DTC checkout across US/UK/AU/CA storefronts. Current live offer mechanics: BUY 2 GET 2 FREE (mix and match), B1G1, and % tiers — see `05-process/creative-brief-process.md` Section C.3 for the hard rule that only these are ever used, never invented.
