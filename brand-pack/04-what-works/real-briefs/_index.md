## Known open flags across this subset (don't resolve silently — stay conservative)
- Feather's live unit-count claims ("6 left," "only 500") are all snapshots in time — default to the honest, evergreen **3-month restock cadence** claim instead of any specific number unless told otherwise.
- Never use a named real public figure (e.g. any celebrity comparison) in ad copy — this was flagged as a legal/right-of-publicity concern in the source corpus (NK141), not a style note.
- Customer/review-count figures vary a lot across the corpus (5,000+, 10,000+, 100,000+ customers) — default to the two confirmed ad-safe figures in `02-personas/00-START-persona-overview.md`: "5,000+ reviews" and "100,000+ happy customers."
