# NK123, NK124, NK128, NK129, NK134, NK141 — LOKI Blackout Content Wave

*Largest single-product cluster in the corpus — 6 batches, all Blackout-focused, spanning Vance and Mike personas.*

## NK123 — "R7B8 LOKI Blackout" (Vance, female VO)
Reuses the hook "Why your husband will be obsessed with THIS knife!" applied to Blackout. 3-reason structure: eye-catching stealth finish → versatility (ribs/veggies/bottle opener) → B2G2 "another knife completely free."

## NK124 — "NORTH_Blackout_S1 Iteration" (Mike register, "king of cookouts")
3-reason structure: insanely sharp → the finger hole ("functional, sure, but let's be honest — it just looks sick") → built-in bottle opener ("One tool to rule the backyard"). Closes: "Tap the link. Become the backyard legend."

## NK128 — "Iteration R9B3" (Mike, fake-study joke)
Comedic fake-science hook ("A new study shows stainless steel knives drop your test[osterone] by 57%. Source: trust me bro."), then pivots to a real claim: **"Over 100,000 men have already made the switch to NorthernKnife."**

## NK129 — "Blackout x Feather" (Mike, cross-sell)
Explicitly cross-sells Blackout and Feather as the two "hero" products in a B2G2 mix-and-match framing — useful reference for any brief that pairs our 2 self-buy/BBQ products together.

## NK134 — "7 Reasons | Long Form" (Mike, longest Blackout script)
Most detailed Blackout feature rundown — 7 numbered reasons (matte finish, finger hole, bottle opener, full tang, hand-forged, razor-sharp out of box, 30-day risk-free trial), closing with **"Over 100,000 happy customers."**

## NK141 — ⚠️ Compliance flag — do not reuse
This batch's hook compared the product to a named real public figure. **Do not reuse that line or any similar celebrity-name comparison in any new copy** — this is a legal/right-of-publicity concern, not a style note. Treat as a hard stop-and-ask item, not approved copy, regardless of how well it might test.

Otherwise a legitimate technique worth noting: the batch tests 3 different orderings of the same 3 reasons (bottle opener / matte finish / finger hole) to see which sequence performs best.

## What this cluster confirms
- Blackout is the most heavily produced single product-variant in the corpus — confirms it as a current priority SKU.
- Never use a named public figure/celebrity in ad copy, for any product.
