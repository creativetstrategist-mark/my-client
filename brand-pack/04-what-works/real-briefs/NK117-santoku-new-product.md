## Distinguishing features vs. Feather (the template this brief is adapted from)
- **Blade style: Santoku** — a different, functionally distinct blade profile from Feather's ornamental feather-shaped blade. **Never reference Japanese origins or the word "Japanese"** in any Santoku copy (per `05-process/creative-brief-process.md` Section E), even though santoku is a Japanese-origin blade shape — this has shipped as a real error before.
- **"Norse patterns etched into the blade"** — pattern-based decoration, distinct from Feather's feather-silhouette pattern.
- **"Dragon-sculpted bolster"** — confirmed **brass** per `01-brand/product-catalog.md`; the bolster is the metal collar between blade and handle.
- **Named as "Bjorn Series"** — a product-line/series name, matching LOKI/ODIN's Norse-god naming convention.
