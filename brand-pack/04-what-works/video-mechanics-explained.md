# Winning Video Ads — Mechanics Index (summary)

*Condensed from the full swipe index — the 5 proven video mechanics, with which are most relevant to our 3 focus products.*

**Historical offer note:** older real ads in the source library use B2G1, not current B2G2 — always swap offer language to whatever's currently live; never copy an old offer mechanic forward as if current.

## 01 — Husband Testimonial / Wife Gifting
Sarah is the explicit speaker/target; Mike/Vance benefit indirectly since the product praise doubles as self-buy proof. The "ironic complaint"/"worst gift" framing (a third party praising, not the brand) reads as social proof rather than a claim — the single most heavily-invested-in format in the account's history.

## 02 — Direct Benefit Listicle
Cold open on a shame/failure frame, then a straight 3-point benefit listicle, closing on identity-reward language. **This is the cleanest, most-proven template for LOKI Blackout specifically** — built around Blackout's two most quotable features (finger-hole grip, built-in bottle opener), Mike persona, no gifting angle, no offer mentioned until the very end.

## 03 — Transparency / Sale-Explanation
"We weren't planning on doing this sale, but here's why we did" — explains real business reasoning behind a discount. Broad/Vance-leaning. Only works when the explanation is genuinely true — never invent a fake reason (factory fire, tariff crisis, etc. must be real if used).

## 04 — Customer Regret / FOMO
Confrontational customer complaint ("I just paid full price") warns the viewer not to repeat the mistake — turns regret into urgency without a countdown timer. General/Mike-adjacent register. Needs a brand-safety pass on tone before reuse (profanity-adjacent in the source).

## 05 — Direct Sale Announcement
Simplest, lowest-production format — plain stated offer with brand identity language, no testimonial/story wrapper. Vance. Useful as a cheap, fast baseline alongside more produced formats.

## What this confirms for our 3 products
- **LOKI Blackout** → mechanic #02 (Direct Benefit Listicle) is the cleanest, most-reused template: shame/failure cold open → sharpness / finger-hole / bottle-opener 3-point listicle → "backyard legend" identity close.
- **Feather Knife / BJORN Santoku** → no dedicated video mechanic in this index (the swipe corpus for these skews static/collector-launch, see `static-formats-explained.md` #09) — when adapting a video inspo ad for these products, lean on the John/Collector voice rules in `05-process/creative-brief-process.md` Section C and E rather than forcing one of the 5 mechanics above.
- All of the above ran under various historical offers — the mechanic transfers, the offer must always be updated to whatever's currently live (B2G2 / B1G1 / % tier).
