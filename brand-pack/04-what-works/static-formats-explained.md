# Winning Static Ads — Format Index (summary)

*Full 12-format breakdown lives in the original pack; kept here as a condensed reference of the formats most relevant to Feather/Santoku (collector-style static) and LOKI Blackout (best-seller static).*

## Format types confirmed to work, with persona fit
1. **Testimonial / UGC Proof** — hero shot + UGC comment cards. Multi-persona (Sarah gift comments + Mike/Steve self-buy comments in one ad). Good for TOF/MOF.
2. **Compared-At** — side-by-side bad-gift-vs-our-knife. Sarah. No urgency/countdown — correctly avoids her biggest turn-off.
3. **Founder's / Transparency Note** — "demand exceeded stock, held pricing instead of raising it" honest-update framing. Broad/Vance-leaning. **Use sparingly** — burns trust if overused (cap: not more than one variant per quarter).
4. **PDP Hero + Offer** — offer stated in the first frame. Vance's #1 rule: never bury the offer.
5. **Gift Reveal / Unboxing** — Sarah + Vance hybrid; reveal moment is the emotional payoff.
6. **Patriotic / Legacy** — legacy/honor language tied to a real calendar occasion, no discount badge needed.
7. **Humor / Reverse-Psychology Listicle** — "Do Not Buy This Knife IF…" joke format. Mike — lets him laugh along instead of feeling sold to.
8. **Before/After Split-Screen** — bad knife vs. hero knife, contrast does the persuasion work visually. Sarah, blunter language than her usual register but framed as her narrating a fact, not an insult at the viewer.
9. **New Product Launch (Feature Callout)** — lead with scarcity/craftsmanship credentials before price ("only 500 units," hand-forged, unique carvings). **This is the standard template for Feather and Santoku launches** — 3-icon trust bar (Hand-Forged / Best Gift / Unique Look), hold the discount for a secondary line. John/Collector primary fit.
10. **Multi-Knife Fan / Podium Showcase** — full-catalog display for cold/prospecting audiences.
11. **Lifestyle Flatlay / Tablescape** — premium styling + a small stated discount. Sarah/Vance hybrid, minimal text.
12. **Quote/Testimonial Lifestyle Hero** — moody masculine hero shot + trust badges (Hand-Forged, Lifetime Warranty, Razor-Sharp). Mike/BBQ King — this is the confirmed visual anchor for Blackout's identity-driven creative.

## Headline rule (applies to every format)
A headline must carry an ANGLE — comparison, insult-then-resolve, curiosity gap, reverse psychology, proof/demonstration, or social stakes. The offer is a badge underneath the headline, never the headline itself. Full detail: `05-process/creative-brief-process.md` Section A.10.
