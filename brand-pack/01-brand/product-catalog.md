## Feather Knife — full detail (Track 1 focus product)
- **Price:** $99.90 (was $149.90, 33% off)
- **Stock: only 6 units available at time of catalog compilation** — real, verifiable scarcity. Independently, `05-process/creative-brief-process.md`'s real-corpus audit (NK130/NK133) confirms a **3-month restock cadence** as the standing, current, honest scarcity detail — prefer stating the restock cadence over a specific live unit count, which goes stale fast.
- Blade: pattern is **laser-applied** (per `05-process/creative-brief-process.md` Section E — never describe the technique as hand-etched/hand-finished/engraved; that has shipped as a factual error before). Hand-forged construction.
- Handle: natural rosewood, full-tang integrated steel shank
- Weight: 260g — notably lighter than standard ~400g knives ("weighs less than your phone" territory) — real UGC-backed angle: lighter reduces wrist fatigue during meal prep (see `04-what-works/real-briefs/NK047-NK048-feather-wrist-pain-ugc.md`)
- Dimensions: 8.27" blade, 13.78" overall length
- 30-day money-back guarantee, lifetime warranty
- **Strategic note:** John's (the Collector persona) signature item alongside BJORN Santoku — see `02-personas/`. John explicitly recoils from discount-first hooks and bundle framing, but the real corpus (NK130/NK133/NK147, the top 3 Feather performers) shows B2G2-tagged Feather briefs performing well — an offer *tag* doesn't mean the offer has to be *stated* in the script itself; scarcity/craft can still lead.

## BJORN Series Santoku — full detail
- Norse engravings etched into the blade, dragon-sculpted **brass** bolster (the metal collar between blade and handle), hand-carved wooden handle.
- **Never reference Japanese origins or the word "Japanese"** anywhere in copy (per `05-process/creative-brief-process.md` Section E) — even though santoku is a Japanese-origin blade shape, the brand's own briefs have shipped this exact error before ("a heavier Japanese chef knife") and it's now a hard rule.
- **Strategic note:** paired with Feather Knife under the JOHN/Collector avatar — both must appear on every JOHN-persona draft.
