# NorthernKnife — Brand Bible
*Reference doc for the Static Ad Creation Project. Built from northernknife.com.*

## What we sell
Hand-forged, Viking-inspired knives across five lines: All-in-One Knives, Bushcraft & Camping, Cooking Knives, BBQ Knives, Collectible Knives — plus sets, sheaths, cutting boards, and sharpeners.

## Core brand lines (use verbatim — these are established brand vocabulary)

- "Excellence is doing ordinary things extraordinarily well" — homepage hero line
- "Inspired by the Vikings, forged for Excellence."
- "Join The Clan" — email capture / community language

## The craftsmanship story (use for JOHN-angle ads — collector, precision, manufacturing complexity)
The brand's standing claim is a traditional, generations-old forging technique: blacksmiths balance carbon content (more carbon = harder/sharper, less carbon = more flexible/solid) by creating two steel blocks, stretching them, and stripping out impurities to homogenize the carbon. Result: a blade that's sharp, flexible, and durable at once — "the perfect combination between sharpness, sturdiness & hardness." Each knife is then hand-assembled. This is the legitimate basis for "hand-forged" and "67 layers of Damascus steel" style claims — it's a real process claim, not just flavor text, so it holds up for the precision-driven JOHN persona.

## Scarcity narrative (JOHN persona — frame as manufacturing complexity, never a deadline)
Limited editions run ~500 pieces. The site's own language: once sold out, blacksmiths may forge a new batch in a few weeks depending on production capacity and demand — or it may never be forged again to protect the collection's prestige. This maps directly onto the existing rule that JOHN's scarcity is about complexity/rarity, never "sale ends Friday."

## Naming system / world-building
Entire catalog uses Norse mythology: Loki, Odin, Ragnar, Ragnarok, Bjorn, Valhalla, Gunnar, plus invented Viking-coded line names (Emberstone, Shadowsteel, Heritage Flame). Headlines lean on conquest/battle verbs: "conquer," "command," "dominate," "unstoppable," "savage sharpness." Useful as a mood-board vocabulary for any visual that needs to feel "in-world" rather than generic DTC.

## Tone of voice
Two registers, blended:
1. **Mythic/heroic** — used in product names and hooks ("Command every cut. Conquer every challenge.")
2. **Warm, reassuring customer service** — used in guarantees and FAQ ("0 Questions Asked," "our lovely team")

Ad copy generally sits in register 1; trust/offer/FAQ copy sits in register 2 — consistent with the existing rule that FAQ copy stays neutral regardless of persona.
