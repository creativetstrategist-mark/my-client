# NorthernKnife — Static Ads SOP & Approved Copy/USP Bank
*Compiled reference for the Static Ad Creation Project.*

## Purpose
Ensures all evergreen ad copy is consistent, on-brand, and matched to the right collection. Defines which copy angle to use depending on knife type, and the duplication rule for best sellers.

## Collections & Rules (relevant to our 3 focus products)

### 🔥 Best Sellers
**Knives:** LOKI Series – Viking Knife, LOKI Series – Blackout Edition
**Selling points:** control-hole design, hand-forged, razor-sharp, versatile all-rounder for kitchen, BBQ, camping, outdoors
**Audience:** men who cook, BBQ pitmasters, campers/bushcraft, women buying gifts

### 🗡️ Collectors & Unique Designs
**Knives (our scope):** BJORN Series (incl. Santoku), Feather Knife
**Selling points:** collectible designs, bold aesthetics, "one-of-a-kind" factor, conversation starters
**Audience:** collectors, fans of unique blades, gifting market, display as much as usage
**Copy angle:** uniqueness, exclusivity, craftsmanship, unique design.

## Usage Sequence
1. Check the knife type first: best seller (Blackout) or collector (Feather/Santoku).
2. Collectors (Feather, Santoku) → highlight uniqueness, artistry, conversation-piece value.
