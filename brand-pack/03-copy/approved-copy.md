# Approved copy — house language

Language the brand actually ships. Use these close to verbatim where marked
**fixed**; treat the rest as register calibration, not as lines to paste.

The originality gate (§F) hard-fails on 6+ consecutive shared words — including
reuse of our own live copy. **The fixed blocks below are the deliberate
exception.** Everything around them gets written fresh, every time.

---

## The proof stack — fixed

```
100,000 customers. Lifetime warranty. 30-day money-back trial. Free returns.
Free shipping.
```

Lands after the CTA, before the parting jab. Drop or reorder elements to fit
pacing; do not embellish them. Shipped variants:

> "100,000 customers. Lifetime warranty. 30-day money-back trial. Free returns."
> "Add two, two more free, your pick. 100,000 customers. Lifetime warranty.
> 30-day money-back. Free returns."

**Never borrow another SKU's review count.** Brand-level claims are portable;
per-product ones are not.

## Offer language

Real offers only: **B2G2 · B2G1 · B1G1 · 64% off · 56% off · 40% + Free Gifts**.
Never invent one. Current storewide: **Buy 2 Get 2 Free + free shipping** —
**verify per campaign, never carry a previous offer over.** No discount codes in
copy.

**How the offer is said** — plainly, as a mechanic, in the customer's arithmetic:

> "What we did instead — buy two, get two free. Whole shop. You pick all four,
> checkout charges you for two."
>
> "So the Blackout stays, I add two, you send two. Five blades, I pay for three?"
>
> "Two knives in the cart, two more free?"
>
> "Four hand-forged blades for what two cost, and nobody's wage got cut."

**Never say "discount" or "sale".** Frame it as a decision the brand made:

> "Instead of hiking prices like everyone else — we made a better call."
> "You'll pay less. The knife hasn't changed."
> "So no, we didn't drop the price. Here's where it actually goes."

**Never explain our own pricing to the viewer** as an argument. Delete on sight:
"let's do the math", "here's the best part".

## Hook patterns

2–3 sentences, ~19–30 words. **The second sentence must turn against the first.**

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Return / reversal | "We had a customer send back his LOKI Viking yesterday. Not because it was bad — because our timing was." |
| Confession | "Yeah, I need to return the Blackout. Nothing wrong with it. It's the best knife I've ever put my hand on." |
| Cost inversion | "You think our knives are expensive? Wait until you hear what the shit one in your drawer is costing you." |
| Withheld secret | "Nobody ever tells you this. The cheapest knife you've ever bought is the most expensive thing in your kitchen." |
| Collector's near-miss | "I almost didn't get one of these. They only made 500." |
| Discovery (Sarah) | "Things I wish I knew about sooner — for my BBQ-obsessed husband." |
| Shame (Mike) | "Swinging a dull kitchen knife at the grill is a recipe for embarrassment." |
| Event (Vance) | "THEY DROPPED A BOMB!" |

The TikTok-comment overlay is a recurring house device: **the comment is the
setup, the hook is the punch.**

---

## Closes

Fixed shape: **CTA → proof stack → parting jab that loops back to the hook.**

Shipped jabs — note each one calls back to its own opening:

> "So yeah — we answered the comment. Go be less right about it."
> "Don't be the guy sending his back. Stack it."

## Register — the house voice

- Blunter and more fragmented than feels safe. Sentence fragments are correct.
- Contractions always.
- Name concrete enemies: **"dollar-store knives"**, never "inferior knives".
- Brand in third person is allowed: *"those crazy hooligans at Northern Knife."*
- Food cuts **"like butter"** — every time.
- Craft, said plainly: *"Every blade we sell gets hammered out by an actual
  blacksmith. Heated, shaped, ground, fitted. One at a time."*
- The material + consequence formula: *"hand-forged from high-carbon steel with
  a razor's edge that stays sharp for months."*

### Delete on sight

- "here's the best part"
- "let's do the math"
- any sentence explaining our own pricing to the viewer
- every banned spec-sheet term in §C.1 — alloy codes, HRC/Rockwell,
  "edge retention", "blade geometry", mm/gram figures

## Per-persona one-liners

The single highest-trust word for each avatar:

| Persona | Word | Why |
|---|---|---|
| JOHN | **insane** (of craft detail) · **heirloom** | Genuine amazement, not polish. Heirloom = the knife outlives the purchase. |
| MIKE | **brutal** | Honesty about the product. Mild profanity works the same way. |
| SARAH | **obsessed** | Highest-converting single word for her. Use it early. |
| VANCE | **legendary** | Bridges the offer and the product — the deal is on something worth having. |
