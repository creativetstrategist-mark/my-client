# Avatars

The live `Avatar` property carries **six** options: **SARAH · MIKE · VANCE ·
JOHN · STEVE · DARIEN**.

## The avatar follows the format, not the product

This is the thing most likely to be got wrong here, because it is the
opposite of how `my-client` works. Observed on the holiday wave:

| Format | Avatar | Cast |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Gift Testimonial | MIKE | Solo man |
| 2 Obsessed Listicle | SARAH | Solo woman |
| 3 Worst Christmas Gift | SARAH | Solo woman |
| 4 Gift Reveal POV | MIKE | Solo man |
| 5 Last-Minute Gift | SARAH | Solo woman |
| 6 I am Mad | VANCE | Solo man |

All four holiday products run through all six formats. **Feather appears with
MIKE, SARAH and VANCE. JOHN appears in none of the holiday wave.** Do not
re-derive an avatar from the product.

Casting at assignment: solo man → Formats 1, 4, 6 · solo woman → 2, 3, 5 ·
couple → any, plus the Couple-cast b-roll.
