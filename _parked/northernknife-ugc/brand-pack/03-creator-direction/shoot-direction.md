# Shoot direction — iPhone only

Every NorthernKnife UGC script must be shootable by **one person, on one
iPhone, with no kit**, in the locations named in the brief. If a beat needs
gear, the beat gets cut. This page is the default direction a batch starts
from; the batch's own direction block (process doc Section E.1) overrides it.

## Defaults

| Field | Default |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Vertical 9:16, 4K if the phone allows, 30fps |
| **Framing** | Handheld, chest height, eye-line **to the lens** |
| **Distance** | Arm's length for talking head; phone propped for two-hand work |
| **Lighting** | Daylight from a window, or open shade outdoors. Overhead lights **off** |
| **Time of day** | Mid-morning or late afternoon. Avoid hard noon sun on a blade |
| **Audio** | Quiet room, phone mic at arm's length. No music under dialogue |
| **Wardrobe** | Plain, unbranded, no busy patterns, no logos |
| **Board & food** | Real wooden board, real food. Nothing staged-perfect |

## Do not

- No gimbal, no slider, no second operator, no ring light, no softbox.
- No digital zoom, no filters, no beauty mode, no speed ramps.
- No music bed under the spoken lines.
- No shot that needs two hands on the phone and two on the knife.

## Shooting a blade on a phone

- **Angle the blade away from the light source** and tilt until the reflection
  breaks — a flat-on blade becomes a white blob.
- The signature detail (pattern, engraving, bolster) needs the phone **close
  and still**: prop it, don't hand-hold.
- Dark handles — ebony on LOKI — lose all definition against a dark surface.
  Shoot ebony against a light board.
- Brass — the BJORN bolster — reads best with soft side light, not front light.

## Safety on camera

- Cuts happen on a board, away from the body, fingers curled.
- Never a cut toward the lens, never a toss, never a spin.
- A visibly unsafe handling shot gets cut regardless of how good it looks.
