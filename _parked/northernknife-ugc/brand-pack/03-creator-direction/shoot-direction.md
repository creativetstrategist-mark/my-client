## Current (from live script pages, Oct 2026)

- Vertical **9:16, 4K**. **Raw files** — no edits, no music, no filters, no
  on-screen text.
- **Every clip 10–20 seconds minimum**, even when the action takes 3. Hold
  before and after.
- Shoot the **A-roll (talking head) and B-roll separately**.
- Film the **three hooks separately from the body** — the edit picks one.
- **Never name a specific delivery cutoff date on camera.** "Before
  Christmas" only.
- Holiday shoot order is a hard constraint: **A set dressing → B wrapping →
  C reveal → D–G in any order**. Wrap **TWO boxes** in Block B, one to open on
  camera and one sealed for pickups. Same sweater, same light, same tree
  through B and C. *The wrapped box is a one-way door.*

## Carried from the Gen 1 shooting-spec callout

- Film in good lighting; don't shoot inside the TikTok app.
- Add 1–3 seconds of handle at the head and tail of every raw clip.
- Turn off ACs, fans and other loud devices.
- **No filters, no colour grading.**
- Avoid clothing with logos; keep the background clean.

## Shooting a blade on a phone

- Angle the blade away from the light and tilt until the reflection breaks —
  flat-on, a blade becomes a white blob.
- Tight detail shots need the phone **close and still**: prop it, don't
  hand-hold.
- Dark handles — ebony on Blackout — lose all definition on a dark surface.
  Shoot ebony against a light board. Brass reads best with soft side light.

## Safety

Cuts happen on a board, away from the body, fingers curled. Never a cut
toward the lens, never a toss, never a spin. A visibly unsafe handling shot
gets cut regardless of how good it looks.
