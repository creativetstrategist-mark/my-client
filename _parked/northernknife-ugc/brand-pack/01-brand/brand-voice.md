## Fixed (from the copy rulebook — already authoritative)

**Plain material + consequence, never the spec sheet.** "Hand-forged
high-carbon steel… holds that edge." Name the real material — rosewood,
ebony, brass — and say what it does for the person holding it.

**Banned, always:** alloy codes, HRC/Rockwell numbers, "edge retention",
"blade geometry", mm/gram specs, "premium craftsmanship",
"precision-engineered", "ergonomic". Full substitution table:
`05-process/ugc-script-process.md` Section C.1.

**Banned in UGC specifically:** "introducing", "look no further", "say goodbye
to", "game-changer", "obsessed", stacked adjectives, any sentence carrying two
ideas.

**Register:** hand-forged, Viking-inspired. Weight and material over
refinement and precision. The brand makes objects people keep, and talks like
it.
