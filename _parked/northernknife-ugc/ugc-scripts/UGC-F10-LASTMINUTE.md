## Filming notes
- Say the knife's name once, at the `[KNIFE]` mark. Don't explain the model — the gift beat is the box and his reaction, not the blade.
- Shoot H1 two or three times naming a different occasion each time. The body never names one, so the edit can retarget without a reshoot.
- Never name a specific delivery cutoff date on camera.
- The offer line sits where it sits. Don't move it to the top.
- **Cast note.** Written for a solo woman, so the package carries no Couple shots and his reaction is covered by hands-only reveals and product b-roll. If the creator can film with a partner, add KNF-GF05, KNF-GF13 and KNF-RX09 and re-tag Cast to Couple.
- **Feather:** never describe how the pattern got onto the blade. Not etched, not hand-finished, not engraved, not laser-applied. It is simply there.
- **BJORN:** never say "Japanese" or reference Japanese origin, tradition or technique. No kanji packaging, no sushi plating.
- **LOKI Blackout:** the bottle opener is in the **handle**.
- No spec-sheet language: no steel grades, no Rockwell, no mm or grams, no "edge retention".

⚠️ **Confirm before shoot:** lifetime warranty, thirty-day money back and free
shipping. A customer/review count is deliberately omitted because the archive
contradicts itself on the figure.
