## Built to be reused — two swappable things

**Any knife.** No knife is named and no model-specific feature is claimed
anywhere in either script. Everything claimed — hand-forged, high-carbon
steel, the branded box with the magnetic lid, warranty and returns — is true
across the lineup. One `[KNIFE]` mark per script.

**Any occasion.** Neither body names an occasion or a season; only the hooks
do. Shoot each hook two or three times with a different occasion.

## Never say, whichever knife is shot

- **Feather** — never describe how the pattern got onto the blade. Not etched,
  not hand-finished, not engraved, not laser-applied. It is simply there.
- **BJORN** — never say "Japanese", and never reference Japanese origin,
  tradition or technique. No kanji packaging, no sushi plating.
- **LOKI Blackout** — the bottle opener is in the **handle**.
- **All knives** — no steel grades, no Rockwell, no mm or grams, no "edge
  retention". No specific delivery cutoff date on camera.

So I got him this. It's the `[KNIFE]`, from Northern Knife. Hand-forged, high-carbon steel, and heavy in a way that makes everything in my kitchen feel like a toy.

Then I found out they're doing buy two, get two free. I got him one and had three left over — so his dad's covered, my brother's covered, and whoever's next.

He has now made every single person who's walked through that door hold it.

Lifetime warranty. Thirty-day money back. Free shipping.

Order it tonight and it'll be there in time.

Or get another gift card, and we both know exactly where that ends up.
