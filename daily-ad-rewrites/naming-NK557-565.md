## Shared across all nine

- **Market `Mkt-All`** — every one of these briefs carries "This runs in the US,
  UK and AU" and bans prices for that reason. They ship to every store unchanged.
- **Event `Evergreen`** — no seasonal hook in any of them.
- **Funnel `Fun-TOF`** — cold prospecting: each opens by establishing the product
  from scratch and points at a 6 Reasons listicle, not a PDP.
- **Category `Adapt` + `Src-`** — every brief has a named brand's ad in AD INSPO
  and states the mechanic it took. See the open question in §D.3 of the process
  doc: this deliberately differs from the Notion `Category` property.
- **Iteration `Ite-None`** — required when Category is Adapt.
- **Creator `Cre-None`** — no talent is cast on any of these yet. **Replace with
  the `Cre-` value at cast time**; this is the one field guessed rather than read.
- **Length `Len-TBD`** — nothing is delivered.

## NK557-559 — The Meat Slice

- **Angle `Product Proof`**: "The mechanism is the cued test… the viewer is
  invited to judge rather than to believe." The ad argues from what the blade does.
- **Structure `Str-Testimonial`**: opens "In my opinion? Worth every bit of it" —
  a first-person owner verdict, then evidence for it.
- **Proof `Prf-Demo`**: "A dull blade shreds this, which is what makes it
  self-verifying." The cut is the proof; the 100,000 line is a CTA stack item, not
  the device.
- **Offer timing `Off-Late`**: the B2G2 beat is block 8 of 9.
- **Product reveal `Rev-Mid`**: "This is the Feather Knife, from Northern Knife"
  is body beat 1, not the hook.
- **Hook tactics**: H1 `Contrarian` — "Every knife looks sharp in an ad";
  H2 `Bold Claim` — "Worth every bit of it"; H3 `Callout` — "This is what *your*
  knife does to meat."
- **NK557 / NK559 `Tone-Blunt`, `Face-Talking`, `Voice-Creator-M`**: "Confident,
  direct, slightly laconic… First-person owner voice"; "No female narrator as
  primary."
- **NK558 `Tone-Serious`, `Face-Hands`**: "Reverent, precise, understated";
  "hands and blade, no presenter face, no chatty delivery."

## NK560-562 — Is It Worth It

- **Structure `Str-Explainer`**: "a big claim → 'is it worth it?' → the breakdown
  → … → the opening question answered."
- **Proof `Prf-Count`**: "Over 100,000 people have bought from them" sits exactly
  where the source puts its million.
- **Offer timing `Off-First`** ⚠️: the arithmetic is body beat 1, inside the first
  ~15 seconds. **This breaks the house rule of Mid or Late** and the field is
  flagging it correctly — in this mechanism the offer *is* the breakdown, so it

- **Tone `Tone-Blunt`**: "Quick, direct, slightly conspiratorial."
- **Voice `Voice-AI-M`** ⚠️ on NK560 and NK561: the brief says "Casting

  **Confirm or switch to `Voice-AI-F`.** NK562 is read from "Deeper VO".
- **Face `Face-Broll`**: people appear (customer photo wall, "mixed hands, mixed
  talent") but nobody speaks to camera.
- **NK560 angle `Cost Logic`**: "The close reframes what 'worth it' means — from
  what four knives cost to how often you replace them." Reveal `Rev-Mid` (the
  feather is named at beat 4).
- **NK561 angle `Versatility`**: "This one interrogates 'the one that does
  everything' — which is the Santoku's actual positioning."
- **NK562 angle `Objection`**: "This one asks whether the blacked-out version is
  just a finish… the doubt this product actually attracts." Reveal `Rev-Early` —
  hook 1C identifies it as "the blacked-out version of their best seller".
- **Hook tactics**: every H1 is `Doubt` (the mechanism opens on the doubt);
  NK560 H2 `Number` / H3 `Command` ("Let's work out whether that's justified");
  NK561 H2 `Contrarian` ("Either that's true or it's marketing") / H3 `Number`;
  NK562 H2 `Confession` ("I assumed it was a paint job") / H3 `Number`.

## NK563-565 — I Don't Work There

- **Angle `Value Offer`**: the argument is the deal — "Buy two and four turn up.
  Any four in the store." The anti-sell is the *tactic* that makes it credible,
  not the argument itself.
- **Structure `Str-Story`**: a recounted scene — "I put four of these in front of
  a friend", then what he asked and what he was told.
- **Offer timing `Off-Mid`**: the B2G2 beat is block 3 of 8, and it arrives "as
  the answer to the ask".
- **Product reveal `Rev-Mid`**: "He went straight for the feather" / "He picked up
  the santoku" / "He went for the Blackout" — body beat 1 in each.
- **Hook tactics**: H1 and H2 both `Story` (both open mid-scene, on the pick and
  on the ask); H3 `Contrarian` — "I don't work for Northern Knife", which is the
  opposite of what an ad says. H3 is the brief's `3D` row.
- **NK563 / NK565 `Tone-Blunt`, `Face-Talking`, `Prf-Count`**: "Plain, unhurried,
  slightly dry"; "Over 100,000 people have bought from them."
- **NK564 `Tone-Serious`, `Face-Hands`, `Prf-Maker`**: "Reverent… no presenter
  face"; and **this cut carries no count at all** — "No volume-proof number on
  this avatar" — so the trust device is the forge and the hand-carving.

# Two things changed while naming

1. **NK564's Landing Page was wrong.** It read `6 Reasons - General`, copied from
   the Feather cut. Both earlier Santoku briefs (NK558, NK561) use
   `6 Reasons - Santoku`. Corrected in Notion, and the name carries `6r-Santoku`.
2. **The awareness letter has nowhere to go.** Our Variant codes (`1C`, `3D`)
   encode hook number *and* awareness band; the new convention's hook field is
   `H1/H2/H3` only. The letter now survives only in the brief's Variant column.
